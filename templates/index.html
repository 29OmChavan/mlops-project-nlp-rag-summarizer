Developer Notes: Building the Summarizer & Hybrid Search Pipeline v2.0.0

When an outage hits, nobody wants to read 80 lines of Slack back-and-forth just to find out which container broke and what fixed it. At the same time, searching internal runbooks with plain dense embeddings fails surprisingly often on short acronyms like `LoRA`, `RRF`, `DVC`, or `KS` because subword tokenizers split unfamiliar acronyms into noisy fragments.

I built this repo in three parts:

1. First-Principles Modules (`first_principles_llm.py`) Implements `BytePairTokenizerFromScratch` over raw UTF-8 byte sequences (0..255), `ScaledDotProductMultiHeadAttention` with upper-triangular `-inf` masking, and `LoRALinearFromScratch` (`W_0 x + (alpha/r) B A x`). Matrix `B` is initialized to zeros so the adapter starts as an exact identity function at step 0.
2. Flan-T5 LoRA Fine-Tuning (`model_trainer.py`) Freezes `google/flan-t5-small` base weights and trains low-rank adapters (`r=8, alpha=16`) on incident transcripts, cutting trainable parameters by ~98.8% and checking ROUGE-1/ROUGE-L gates before saving.
3. Qdrant + BM25 RRF (`rag_engine.py`) Runs acronym query expansion, queries both Qdrant cosine vectors and BM25 Okapi lexical scores, combines ranks via Reciprocal Rank Fusion (`1/(60 + r_dense) + 1/(60 + r_bm25)`), and checks every sentence in the output for content-word overlap against the retrieved `[DOC-ID]` chunks.
Help & Operator Guide: How to Use Every Tab User Manual

1. Summarizing Incident Logs (Tab 1, Left Panel)

  • Click one of the three Preset buttons in the blue bar at the top, or paste any multi-speaker chat log into the transcript box.
  • Pick a format style (Executive Brief, SRE Post-Mortem, or SLA Audit Note) and click Run LoRA Summarizer.
  • Check the Sentence Grounding Check at the bottom of the panel: sentences with `>= 65%` content-word overlap with your source transcript get a green `VERIFIED_GROUNDED` badge.

2. Comparing Naive Dense vs. BM25 vs. Hybrid RRF (Tab 1, Right Panel)

  • Enter a technical question (try including acronyms like `LoRA`, `RRF`, or `KS`) and click Search & Compare 3 Modes.
  • Look at the 3-column table under Side-by-Side Retriever Comparison to see which `[DOC-ID]` chunks Naive Dense, Pure BM25, and Hybrid RRF ranked #1, #2, and #3.

3. Indexing New Runbooks & Testing BPE / LoRA Math (Tabs 2 & 3)

  • In Tab 2, fill out the right-hand form and click Index Chunk Now to add a new `[DOC-xxx]` entry into Qdrant and BM25.
  • In Tab 3, test custom `{context}` / `{query}` prompt templates on the left, or inspect the UTF-8 byte-pair merge table and LoRA parameter reduction calculator on the right.
